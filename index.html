My Notes Notifier · Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

My Notes Notifier ("the app") is a reminder and alarm app published by NANO SOLUTIONS ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Your reminders stay on your phone.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The only data that leaves your phone automatically is anonymous diagnostic data: crash reports, and when the app is opened. It lets us fix bugs.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your reminders, notes, times and repeat settings
  • your history of done, skipped and missed reminders
  • your holidays and settings
  • supply and refill counts

It also keeps any sounds you add. When you pick music, the app copies the file into its own storage. When you record your voice, the recording is saved in the app's storage. Neither is uploaded anywhere.

We, the developers, cannot see any of this information.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap "Record your voice" to create a reminder sound. The recording is saved on your phone and used only as that reminder's sound. The app never records in the background.

Voice typing. If you tap the microphone next to a reminder's title, your phone's own speech service (usually Google's) turns your voice into text. The app receives only the text. That service handles your voice under its own privacy policy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Google Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains:

  • technical details of the error
  • your phone model and Android version
  • the app version
  • a random installation identifier

Crashlytics also records when the app is opened, with the phone model and connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data). This lets us see what share of people use the app without crashes.

None of this contains your reminders, notes or recordings. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. Google processes them under the Firebase privacy terms.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: your reminders, history, sounds and settings stay until you delete them in the app, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes them from your phone.
  • Crash reports: Firebase keeps them for about 90 days, then deletes them. They are not linked to your name or account. To ask us about them, write to the email below.
  • Backups: a backup file is yours to keep or delete. Android backup can be turned off, and its data removed, in your phone's Google settings.

Security

Crash reports are encrypted while they travel. Everything else stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

Backups

Android backup. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may copy the app's data (reminders, history, holidays, settings and voice recordings) to your own Google account. Google handles this under your account settings. We never receive it.

Backup files. When you choose "Export to a file", the app writes a backup file to the place you pick, for example your phone or Google Drive. That file is under your control.

Email support

If you tap "Email support", your email app opens with a message to us. The message includes:

  • the app version
  • your phone model and Android version
  • whether alarm permissions are allowed
  • how many reminders you have

You can read and edit all of it before sending. We use it only to answer you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
